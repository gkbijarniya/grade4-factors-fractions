FACTORS

What is a factor?

A factor is a whole number that divides another number exactly, with no remainder.

Example: 3 is a factor of 12 because 12 ÷ 3 = 4.

So the factors of 12 are:

1 · 2 · 3 · 4 · 6 · 12
FACTOR PAIRS

Find factor pairs

Multiply two whole numbers to make the target number.

For 24:
1 × 24
2 × 12
3 × 8
4 × 6

That gives the factor pairs of 24.

COMMON FACTORS

Compare two numbers

Factors that two numbers share are called common factors.

Factors of 12: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12
Factors of 18: 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18
Common factors: 1, 2, 3, 6
REMEMBER

Quick tips

  • 1 is a factor of every whole number.
  • Every number is a factor of itself.
  • A factor always divides evenly.
  • Factors come in pairs when multiplied.
FRACTIONS

What is a fraction?

A fraction shows equal parts of a whole.

🍕 🍕 🍕 🍕
34 of the pizza is shaded.

The numerator is on top. The denominator is on the bottom.

TYPES

Types of fractions

  • Proper: numerator is smaller than denominator, e.g. 3/5.
  • Improper: numerator is equal to or greater than denominator, e.g. 7/4.
  • Mixed number: a whole number and a fraction, e.g. 1 3/4.
EQUIVALENT

Equivalent fractions

Equivalent fractions have the same value even though their numbers look different.

12 = 24 = 36

Multiply or divide the numerator and denominator by the same non-zero number.

COMPARE

Which fraction is bigger?

When denominators are the same, compare the numerators.

3/8 > 1/8 because 3 parts are more than 1 part.

When denominators differ, you can use equivalent fractions or compare the sizes of the parts.
